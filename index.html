English Course

Learn Step by Step: A1 to C1

A structured progression from beginner English to advanced communication.

A1

Beginner

Greetings, introductions, numbers, basic grammar, daily conversations, and foundational vocabulary.

A2

Elementary

Past and future forms, travel, weather, health, shopping, and everyday communication.

B1

Intermediate

Perfect tenses, storytelling, expressing opinions, and practical conversations.

B2

Upper Intermediate

Passive voice, reported speech, conditionals, interviews, emails, and meetings.

C1

Advanced

Idioms, advanced vocabulary, accent development, debate, and advanced communication.
